## Specialized life areas

CosmyDay's public prose contains actual labeled sections: daily Overview, Work & goals, Relationships, Energy & mood; weekly Work & goals and Relationships; monthly Career & ambition, Love & relationships, Money & practical matters, Wellbeing. These are sections inside one horoscope text, not separately documented category endpoints. Their bounds and editorial consistency need validation before exposing them as distinct category readings. FreeHoroscopeAPI's checked response has only one general `horoscope` field. Prokerala documents daily general/love/career/health types, but not an affordable full-period solution. No provider here verifies Muse's entire requested category matrix across all three periods.

## Fit and safe fallback

CosmyDay and FreeHoroscopeAPI need only a sign and period, so a server-side adapter could serve both the authenticated Observatory and Daily Edit without transmitting birth data. The app must validate the **content's** date/range against the provider's documented timezone, not use HTTP status or cache headers as the freshness test. CosmyDay supplied `generated_at`; the sampled responses had no Cache-Control, ETag or Last-Modified headers. FreeHoroscopeAPI sampled responses had no Cache-Control header and showed a Cloudflare `EXPIRED` response despite date mismatches. A failed, missing, stale or unsupported category must show an honest unavailable state. Any older cached reading could only be labeled and shown if explicit licensing allows it; do not silently relabel it as today's reading.

## Remaining decision and evidence

1. Ask CosmyDay for written permission covering authenticated display, private saved-forecast snapshots, export, offline PWA storage, cache lifetime and attribution placement. Confirm whether its AI-assisted editorial process is acceptable for Muse.
2. Check all 12 signs across at least one actual daily rollover, a weekly boundary and a monthly boundary. Record content labels, `generated_at`, hashes, failures and response times. One short session verifies current coverage, not ongoing reliability.
3. Keep FreeHoroscopeAPI as the fallback candidate only if its stale/mixed-date behavior is fixed and its cache/archive, source, quota and refresh terms are clarified.
4. Do not activate a provider, create accounts/trials, expose birth details, buy subscriptions or migrate hosted data based on this audit.
